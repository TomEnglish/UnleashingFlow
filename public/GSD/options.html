Options Comparison

Build vs. buy analysis for Grand Slam Dumpster Rentals. All costs assume AI-assisted development at $150/hr and 30% annual business growth unless noted.

Recommendation: Path B (Hybrid). Custom AI for inbox, voice, and owner's-voice email — iCans Plus for dispatch, driver app, and QuickBooks sync. Lowest execution risk with most of the long-term TCO advantage.

Key insights

  • AI dev productivity cut build costs ~75% vs traditional estimates — what was $35K–$70K is now $10K–$20K.
  • LLM deflation is real but partial — only 23–42% of opex; Twilio and QB stay flat or rise.
  • Business growth is the bigger lever — SaaS scales linearly; custom infra stays mostly fixed.
  • Build now vs. wait 6 months — operating value of 6 months likely exceeds ~$4K saved on cheaper models later.
  • iCans pricing is opaque at scale — validate with direct vendor quote before committing.
